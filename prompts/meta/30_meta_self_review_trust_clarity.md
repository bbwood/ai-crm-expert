# Meta: Self-Review for Trust and Clarity

## Purpose

Before returning your final message, evaluate its quality against key criteria to ensure it meets high standards for customer communication.

## Evaluation Criteria

Rate the message you just created on a scale of 1-10 for each criterion:

### 1. Clarity (1-10)
- Is the message easy to understand?
- Is the action requested clear?
- Are there any confusing or ambiguous phrases?
- Would the average person immediately understand what you're asking?

### 2. Trust and Tone (1-10)
- Does it sound like advice from a trusted friend?
- Is it free from sales pressure or manipulation?
- Does it respect the customer's autonomy?
- Would you be comfortable receiving this message?

### 3. Retention Impact (1-10)
- Does it strengthen the relationship?
- Does it provide genuine value?
- Would this make them more likely to return?
- Does it reinforce the shop's expertise and care?

## Self-Review Process

1. **Score Each Criterion**: Assign honest scores (1-10) for Clarity, Trust/Tone, and Retention Impact.

2. **Identify Issues**: If ANY score is below 8, identify specific problems:
   - What word choices feel off?
   - What could be clearer?
   - What might damage trust?

3. **Revise Once**: Make targeted improvements to address scores below 8.

4. **Return Final Version**: Output only the improved message with your final scores.

## Output Format

Return your response as:

```
[Final Message Text]

---
SELF-REVIEW SCORES:
- Clarity: X/10
- Trust & Tone: X/10
- Retention Impact: X/10
```

## Standards

- Messages scoring 8+ across all criteria are ready to send
- Messages with any score below 8 must be revised
- Only revise once - trust your professional judgment
- Be honest in your self-assessment

## Common Issues to Watch For

- Generic or templated language (lowers Trust)
- Unclear next steps (lowers Clarity)
- Pressure tactics or urgency manipulation (lowers Trust)
- Too long or verbose (lowers Clarity and Retention)
- Not personalized enough (lowers Trust and Retention)
- Missing context from customer data (lowers Retention)
