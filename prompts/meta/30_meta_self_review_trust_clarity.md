# Meta: Self-Review for Trust and Clarity

## Purpose

Before returning your final message, evaluate its quality against key criteria to ensure it meets high standards for customer communication. BE CRITICAL - most messages should score between 5-8, not 9-10.

## Evaluation Criteria

Rate the message you just created on a scale of 1-10 for each criterion using the DEDUCTION SYSTEM below. Start at 10 and subtract points for each issue.

### 1. Clarity (Start at 10, deduct points)

**Deduct 2 points for EACH:**
- Unclear or vague call-to-action ("let me know" vs. specific phone number/action)
- Ambiguous pronouns or references (unclear what "it" or "that" refers to)
- Technical jargon without explanation
- Sentence longer than 25 words

**Deduct 1 point for EACH:**
- Missing contact information or next step
- Passive voice instead of active ("was checked" vs. "we checked")
- Unclear timeline ("soon" vs. "within 2 weeks")
- More than 4 sentences in a paragraph

**Score Ranges:**
- 9-10: Perfectly clear, immediately actionable, under 75 words
- 7-8: Clear but minor improvements possible (slightly wordy or one vague phrase)
- 5-6: Understandable but has clarity issues (unclear CTA, too long, or jargon)
- 3-4: Multiple clarity problems, requires effort to understand
- 1-2: Confusing or unclear

### 2. Trust and Tone (Start at 10, deduct points)

**Deduct 3 points for EACH:**
- Any sales pressure or urgency manipulation ("act now," "limited time")
- Guilt-tripping language ("you should have," "it's been too long")
- Fear-based messaging without factual basis

**Deduct 2 points for EACH:**
- Generic template language ("Hope this finds you well," "reaching out to")
- Missing personalization when customer data is available (name, vehicle, service history)
- Over-familiar tone for first interaction ("buddy," "my friend")
- Defensive or excuse-making language

**Deduct 1 point for EACH:**
- Sounds more like marketing than helpful advice
- Uses shop name excessively (more than once)
- Missing service advisor's name when appropriate
- Emojis or excessive punctuation (!!!, ???)

**Score Ranges:**
- 9-10: Perfectly trusted friend tone, fully personalized, zero pressure
- 7-8: Good tone but slight marketing feel or missing minor personalization
- 5-6: Acceptable but generic template language or mild pressure detected
- 3-4: Sales-y tone, lacks trust, pressure tactics present
- 1-2: Manipulative or inappropriate

### 3. Retention Impact (Start at 10, deduct points)

**Deduct 2 points for EACH:**
- No value provided (just asking for money without helpful context)
- Missing opportunity to reference positive past experience
- Doesn't demonstrate expertise or care from the shop
- Generic message that could be sent to anyone

**Deduct 1 point for EACH:**
- Doesn't strengthen relationship or add value
- Missing context about why this matters to THIS customer
- No acknowledgment of customer loyalty/history
- Doesn't show shop personality or values
- Could have been more timely or relevant

**Score Ranges:**
- 9-10: Exceptional - highly personalized, valuable, strengthens relationship significantly
- 7-8: Good value, mostly personalized, solid relationship building
- 5-6: Acceptable but generic, limited relationship impact
- 3-4: Low value, transactional only, doesn't build relationship
- 1-2: Could damage relationship

## Self-Review Process

1. **Apply Deduction System**: Start each criterion at 10, subtract points for specific issues found

2. **Be Brutally Honest**: Most messages should score 5-8, not 9-10. A score of 9+ means the message is nearly perfect.

3. **Mandatory: Identify 1-2 Areas for Improvement**: Even if scores are high, identify what could make the message better. This is REQUIRED.

4. **Revise Once**: If any score is below 6, revise the message before returning it.

5. **Return Final Version**: Output the message with scores AND improvement areas.

## Output Format

Return your response as:

```
[Final Message Text]

---
SELF-REVIEW SCORES:
- Clarity: X/10
- Trust & Tone: X/10
- Retention Impact: X/10

AREAS FOR IMPROVEMENT:
- [What would make this message score higher? Be specific.]
- [Even perfect 10s can identify theoretical improvements]
```

## Standards

- **9-10 (Exceptional)**: Nearly flawless, highly personalized, perfect execution
- **7-8 (Good)**: Solid message, minor improvements possible, ready to send
- **5-6 (Acceptable)**: Gets the job done but generic or has notable issues, should revise
- **Below 5 (Unacceptable)**: Must revise before sending

## BE CRITICAL

Your job is NOT to give high scores - it's to identify real improvements. Most messages are 6-7, not 9-10. Challenge yourself to find at least 2 areas for improvement in every message.
