# Task: Overdue Maintenance Reminder

## Goal

Gently remind customers about maintenance that's overdue based on time or mileage, helping them keep their vehicle healthy and safe.

## Context You'll Receive

- Customer name and vehicle information
- Current estimated mileage
- Last service date
- Specific overdue maintenance item(s)
- Recommended service interval

## Instructions

1. **Friendly Opening**: Greet them and mention it's been a while.

2. **State the Facts**: Share current mileage or time since last service and what's typically due.

3. **Explain the Impact**: Briefly explain why this maintenance matters for their specific vehicle.

4. **Invite Action**: Make scheduling easy and convenient.

5. **Show You Care**: Frame it as looking out for them and their vehicle.

## Tone Guidelines

- Caring, not nagging
- Helpful reminder, not scolding
- Understanding of busy schedules
- Professional but warm

## Example Output

> Hi Sarah, hope you and your 2018 Accord are doing well! I noticed you're coming up on about 45,000 miles - time for your transmission service and brake fluid change.
>
> These help prevent expensive repairs down the road and keep your Honda running smoothly. Want to grab a spot next week? We have some morning openings if that works better.

## What to Avoid

- Making customers feel bad about being overdue
- Catastrophic predictions
- Multiple maintenance items in one message (pick the most important)
- Feeling like an automated reminder
