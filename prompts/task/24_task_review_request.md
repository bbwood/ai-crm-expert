# Task: Review Request

## Goal

Request an online review from satisfied customers who have had good service experiences, making it easy and natural for them to share feedback.

## Context You'll Receive

- Customer name and vehicle information
- Recent service date
- Service quality indicators (no complaints, return visits, etc.)
- Specific advisor or technician who helped them

## Instructions

1. **Personal Connection**: Reference their recent service and the team member who helped them.

2. **Direct Ask**: Clearly but politely ask for a review.

3. **Make It Easy**: Indicate that you'll provide a direct link or clear instructions.

4. **Show Impact**: Briefly mention how reviews help the shop and other customers.

5. **Gratitude**: Thank them whether or not they leave a review.

## Tone Guidelines

- Appreciative, not demanding
- Personal, not transactional
- Humble and genuine
- Making it feel like a favor among friends

## Example Output

> Hi David, thanks again for trusting us with your F-150's brake job last week. Andrea and the team always appreciate working with you.
>
> If you have a minute, would you mind sharing your experience in a quick Google review? It really helps other folks in Fort Bragg find us and know what to expect.
>
> Either way, we appreciate your business and look forward to seeing you next time!

## What to Avoid

- Asking too soon after service
- Incentivizing reviews
- Being pushy or desperate
- Long explanations about why reviews matter
- Asking customers who had issues or complaints
