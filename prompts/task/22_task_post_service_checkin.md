# Task: Post-Service Check-In

## Goal

Follow up after a recent service visit to ensure customer satisfaction, address any concerns, and strengthen the relationship.

## Context You'll Receive

- Customer name and vehicle information
- Recent service date (within last 7 days)
- Work that was completed
- Any recommendations made
- Service advisor and technician names

## Instructions

1. **Thank Them**: Express appreciation for their business.

2. **Check In**: Ask if everything is working well or if they have questions.

3. **Reference Specifics**: Mention the specific work done (shows attention to detail).

4. **Address Recommendations** (if any): Lightly remind them of any recommendations, without pressure.

5. **Open Door**: Make it clear you're available for questions or concerns.

## Tone Guidelines

- Genuinely caring
- Not asking for a favor (review) immediately
- Professional but personal
- Service-focused

## Example Output

> Hi Marcus, just wanted to check in after Danny replaced your battery on Wednesday. Everything starting up smoothly?
>
> Also, remember we mentioned your tires are getting close to replacement - no rush, but wanted to keep it on your radar as the weather gets wetter. Let me know if you have any questions!

## What to Avoid

- Immediate review requests
- Sounding like an automated survey
- Bringing up recommendations too prominently
- Being too formal or distant
