# Task: Post-Service Check-In

## Goal

Follow up after a recent service visit to ensure customer satisfaction, address any concerns, and strengthen the relationship.

## Context You'll Receive

- Customer name and vehicle information
- Recent service date (within last 7 days)
- Work that was completed
- Any recommendations made
- Service advisor and technician names

## Instructions

**STEP 1 - CHECK TIME REFERENCE FIRST**: Before writing anything, determine how long ago the service was:
- Calculate days since service date
- Select the EXACT phrase to use based on this table:

| Days Ago | REQUIRED Time Reference |
|----------|-------------------------|
| 0-1 | "today" or "earlier today" |
| 2-3 | Specific day name ("on Monday", "on Tuesday") |
| 4-7 | "last week" |
| 8-14 | "last week" or "a week ago" |
| 15-30 | "a few weeks ago" or "last month" |
| 31-60 | "last month" |
| 61-90 | "a couple months ago" or month name ("back in March") |
| 90+ | Month name ("back in August") or season |

**CRITICAL**: You MUST NOT use specific day names (Monday, Tuesday, Wednesday, etc.) for visits more than 7 days ago. This sounds weird and confusing to customers.

**STEP 2 - Write the Message**:

1. **Thank Them**: Express appreciation for their business.

2. **Check In with CORRECT time reference**: Ask if everything is working well, using the time reference you determined in Step 1.

3. **Reference Specifics**: Mention the specific work done (shows attention to detail).

4. **Address Recommendations** (if any): Lightly remind them of any recommendations, without pressure.

5. **Open Door**: Make it clear you're available for questions or concerns.

## Tone Guidelines

- Genuinely caring
- Not asking for a favor (review) immediately
- Professional but personal
- Service-focused

## Example Outputs

**Recent visit (2-3 days ago):**
> Hi Marcus, just wanted to check in after Danny replaced your battery on Monday. Everything starting up smoothly?
>
> Also, remember we mentioned your tires are getting close to replacement - no rush, but wanted to keep it on your radar as the weather gets wetter. Let me know if you have any questions!

**Visit 3-4 weeks ago (15-30 days) - CORRECT:**
> Hi Gadge, just wanted to check in after your service a few weeks ago. How's the Ram running after the oil change and engine treatment?
>
> Wes mentioned your front and rear brakes are getting close - no emergency, but worth planning for in the next month or two. Happy to answer any questions!

**Visit 3-4 weeks ago (15-30 days) - WRONG (DO NOT DO THIS):**
> ❌ Hi Gadge, just wanted to check in after your service on Wednesday. [WRONG - don't use day names for 28 days ago!]

**Visit 1-2 months ago (31-60 days):**
> Hi Marcus, wanted to check in on your Ram since we replaced the battery last month. Still starting up strong?
>
> Also, just keeping those tires on your radar that we mentioned - no immediate rush, but worth planning for in the next few months. Let me know if you have questions!

## What to Avoid

- Immediate review requests
- Sounding like an automated survey
- Bringing up recommendations too prominently
- Being too formal or distant
