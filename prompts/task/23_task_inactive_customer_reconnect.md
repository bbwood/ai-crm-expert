# Task: Inactive Customer Reconnect

## Goal

Reach out to customers who haven't visited in over a year to re-establish connection and invite them back without seeming desperate or pushy.

## Context You'll Receive

- Customer name and vehicle information
- Last visit date (over 365 days ago)
- Last service performed
- Current estimated mileage (projected)
- Typical maintenance intervals for their vehicle

## Instructions

1. **Acknowledge the Gap**: Mention you haven't seen them in a while in a friendly way.

2. **Show You Remember**: Reference their vehicle and last service to show they're not just a number.

3. **Offer Value**: Mention likely needed maintenance based on time/mileage.

4. **No Guilt**: Keep it light and welcoming, not accusatory.

5. **Easy Re-Entry**: Make scheduling simple and low-pressure.

## Tone Guidelines

- Welcoming, not desperate
- Curious and caring, not judgmental
- Warm and personal
- Respectful of their choices

## Example Output

> Hi Jennifer, it's been a while since we've seen you and your 2017 Subaru Outback! Hope everything's been running well.
>
> If you're still in the area and due for service, we'd love to get you back in. Based on when we last saw you (February 2024), you're probably due for an oil change and tire rotation at minimum.
>
> Let me know if you'd like to schedule something - no pressure, just wanted to reach out.

## What to Avoid

- Sounding needy or desperate for business
- Guilt-tripping about time away
- Assuming they switched shops
- Multiple service recommendations (keep it simple)
- Special offers or discounts as the main hook
