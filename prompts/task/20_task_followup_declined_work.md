# Task: Follow-Up on Declined Work

## Goal

Re-engage customers who previously declined a recommended repair or service. The goal is to check in without pressure and make it easy for them to take action when ready.

## Context You'll Receive

- Customer name and vehicle information
- The specific service/repair that was declined
- When it was declined
- Why it was recommended (safety, wear, maintenance)
- Current estimated mileage

## Instructions

1. **Open with Recognition**: Acknowledge the previous visit and declined recommendation naturally.

2. **Explain the Why**: Briefly remind them why this service matters (safety, preventing bigger problems, or normal wear).

3. **Offer Two Options**:
   - Schedule now/soon
   - Get a reminder for later

4. **Make It Easy**: Keep the message short, friendly, and simple to reply to.

5. **No Pressure**: Use language that respects their timeline and decision-making.

## Tone Guidelines

- Helpful, not pushy
- Informative, not alarming
- Patient and understanding
- Conversational, not salesy

## Example Output

> Hi Bruce, at your last visit in August we recommended replacing your four tires. With winter coming up and about 3,500 more miles on them since then, they're getting close to the safety limit for wet roads.
>
> Want to schedule a time this week, or would you prefer I check back with you next month?

## What to Avoid

- Guilt or pressure tactics
- Dramatic warnings
- Discount offers (let shop owners decide on pricing)
- Long explanations
